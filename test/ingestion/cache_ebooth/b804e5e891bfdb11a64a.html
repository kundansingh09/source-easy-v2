COMPANY PROFILE Aimil Ltd. was incorporated in the year 1932 to cater to the instrumentation needs of the country. Today, Aimil is a multifunctional, multi-service organisation in the instrumentation industry. Aimil has built its presence in the Instrumentation domain in the Indian market. Aimil is a leading ISO 9001:2015 certified Company having an All-India network of 16 offices, staffed and managed by over 1000 + professionals, 400 of whom are trained engineering graduates with rich and varied experience in the instrumentation industry. We specialize in marketing of state-of-the-art instruments in the fields of: • Building Material Testing • Survey & Geospatial • Geophysical • Non-Destructive Testing • Structural Monitoring • Pavement Engineering • Traffic Monitoring & Safety • Civil Engineering Software • RF & Microwave • Noise Vibration & Harshness • Optronics • Environment Simulation & Solutions • Power Electronics • Electricals • Data Acquisitions • Condition Monitoring & Alignment • Particle Size, Dissolution • Industrial Material Testing • Cryogenics characterization • Coating & Inspection • Contamination Control • Healthcare and Medical Instrumentation We also offer consultancy in the fields of Civil Engineering, Noise, Vibration & Engineering Services and Condition Monitoring Services. We do justice to our range via our unique management structure which operates through independent product group teams located at our various offices, integrated via National Managers who integrate product-wise operations on a nationwide basis. To realise our organisational goal of delivering state-of-the-art technology to the Indian marketplace, we represent several organisations from the UK, US, Europe and Japan. Please visit our website www.aimil.com for further details.
